## 1. Overview & Immediate Response Protocols
When water leakage is reported in a residential property, emergency classification must occur immediately to prevent catastrophic structural weakening and electrical fire risks.

### Immediate Action Checklist:
1. **Water Isolation:** Shut off the main water isolation stopcock immediately.
2. **Electrical Hazard Mitigation:** If water pooling is near electrical outlets, baseboard wiring, or kitchen appliances, immediately trip the corresponding circuit breaker in the consumer distribution board.
3. **Photo/Video Documentation:** The property representative must capture clear high-resolution photographic evidence showing:
   - Point of origin (pipe joint, valve, fixture)
   - Extent of water spreading and depth of pooling
   - Visible damage to adjacent drywall, masonry, or flooring
4. **Emergency Inspection:** Dispatch a certified plumbing specialist for on-site inspection within 4 hours for HIGH severity incidents.

## 2. Leakage Categorization & Typical Defects
- **Category 1: Clean Water Leak (Supply Line)**
  - Source: Pressurized PPR/CPVC supply pipes, sink flex hoses, faucet cartridges.
  - Risk: Rapid pooling, wall seepage, subfloor damage.
  - Remediation: Pipe segment replacement, compression coupling, pressure testing at 6 bar for 30 minutes.
- **Category 2: Greywater Leak (Drainage / Trap)**
  - Source: P-traps, sink waste pipes, dishwasher drain hoses.
  - Risk: Odor, mold formation, bacterial contamination.
  - Remediation: Waste assembly replacement, sealing sleeves, sanitization.
- **Category 3: Concealed In-Wall Leak**
  - Source: Joint failure inside brick/block masonry or tile screed.
  - Indicators: Efflorescence, bubbling paint, damp smell, acoustic detection.
  - Remediation: Non-destructive acoustic tracing, targeted plaster opening (max 500x500mm), pipe replacement, structural moisture drying, water-resistant plastering and repainting.

## 4. Minimum Quality & Warranty Guidelines
- All plumbing repair work must provide a minimum warranty period of **6 to 12 months** on workmanship and **5 years** on manufacturer-warranted piping materials.
- Testing certificate / pressure gauge reading photo must be attached prior to job completion sign-off.
