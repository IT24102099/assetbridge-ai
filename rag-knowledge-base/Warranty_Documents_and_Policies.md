# AssetBridge AI — Warranty Policies & Vendor Accountability Framework
**Document ID:** KB-DOC-WAR-006  
**Category:** Legal, Warranty & Service Quality Assurance  

---

## 1. Warranty Classification
1. **Manufacturer Warranty:** Valid on major hardware (water pumps, inverter AC units, solar inverters, CPVC piping). Covers material flaws for 3 to 10 years.
2. **Workmanship Warranty:** Provided directly by verified service providers for repair and installation labor.
   - Minor repairs (fixtures, traps): Minimum 3 months.
   - Major concealed plumbing / pipe replacement: Minimum **12 months**.
   - Masonry waterproofing & roof repairs: Minimum **24 months**.
   - Electrical distribution board upgrades: Minimum **12 months**.

---

## 2. Warranty Claims & Dispute Resolution
- If failure occurs within the warranty window, the assigned provider is legally bound under the AssetBridge Provider Agreement to conduct remedial repairs within 48 hours at zero additional cost to the owner.
- AI Agent 3 flags any quotation that does not provide warranty terms or offers shorter warranty periods compared to industry baselines.
