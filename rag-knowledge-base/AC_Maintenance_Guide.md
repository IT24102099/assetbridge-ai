## 1. Typical Faults & Inspection Protocols
- **Water Dripping from Indoor Unit:** Blocked condensate drain pipe, algae buildup, uninsulated suction pipe. Clear line with nitrogen purge or suction pump. Standard cost: LKR 4,500 – LKR 7,000.
- **Inadequate Cooling:** Refrigerant leak (R32 / R410A), dirty evaporator coil, faulty thermistor. Full chemical wash & vacuum recharge cost: LKR 10,000 – LKR 14,000.
- **Compressor Overload Tripping:** Low voltage, high ambient condenser temperature, capacitor failure.
- **Required Warranty:** Minimum 6 months on gas recharging and coil repair.
