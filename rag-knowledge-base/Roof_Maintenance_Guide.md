# AssetBridge AI — Roof & Structural Waterproofing Guide
**Document ID:** KB-DOC-ROOF-008  
**Category:** Structural, Roofing, Waterproofing  

---

## 1. Inspection Points
- Displaced or cracked clay roof tiles (Sinhala / Calicut / Roman tiles).
- Deteriorated valley gutters, lead flashing cracks near parapet walls.
- Flat concrete roof slab ponding water and hairline shrinkage cracks.

---

## 2. Standard Works & Pricing Benchmarks
- **Roof Tile Re-alignment & Valley Gutter Cleaning:** LKR 15,000 – LKR 22,000.
- **Elastomeric Waterproof Coating (2 Coats with Fiber Mesh):** LKR 450 – LKR 650 per sq. ft.
- **Parapet Wall Flashing Re-bedding with Waterproof Mortar:** LKR 12,000 – LKR 18,000.
- **Warranty Requirement:** Minimum 24 months leak-free warranty for elastomeric coating.
