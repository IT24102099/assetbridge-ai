# AssetBridge AI — Comprehensive Property Maintenance Manual
**Document ID:** KB-DOC-GEN-005  
**Scope:** Remote Asset Management for Overseas Property Owners  

---

## 1. Preventive Maintenance Lifecycle
Overseas owners rely on systematic lifecycle audits to preserve capital value and prevent sudden emergency dilapidation.

1. **Quarterly Inspections:** Representative conducts visual and plumbing leak checks, dampness detection, and roof gutters inspection.
2. **Semi-Annual Services:** HVAC air filter deep clean, electrical switchboard thermal imaging, water pump motor lubrication.
3. **Annual Audits:** Exterior wall waterproof coating inspection, pest barrier renewal, rainwater drainage clearing before monsoon season.

---

## 2. Maintenance Workflow Standards
When an incident is reported:
1. **Inspection within 24 Hours:** Authorized local representative visits the property, inspects with calibrated moisture meters/testing tools, records findings, and attaches timestamped photos.
2. **Quotation Generation (Minimum 2–3 Quotes):** Trusted, verified service providers from the AssetBridge network submit itemized quotes (materials, labour, testing).
3. **AI Comparison & Recommendation:** Agent 3 cross-references owner budget, service history, price books, provider ratings, and warranty conditions to formulate an optimal proposal.
4. **Owner / Manager Approval:** High-impact expenditures are deterministically checked and presented for one-click approval on the React / Flutter dashboard.
5. **Supervised Execution & Sign-off:** Representative supervises work on-site, validates completion photos, confirms pressure/safety tests, and updates the asset continuity ledger.
