## 1. Electrical Hazards Involving Water Intrusion
Water conducts electricity and creates lethal ground faults, arc flashes, and structural fires. When moisture or standing water interacts with electrical infrastructure:

### Mandatory Emergency Protocols:
1. **Critical Warning:** Water near electrical equipment poses immediate risk of electrocution.
2. **Immediate Action:** **Isolate Electrical Supply Immediately**.
   - Trip the Main Circuit Breaker or specific Residual Current Circuit Breaker with Overcurrent protection (RCBO).
   - If the electrical distribution panel is located in a submerged or damp area, DO NOT approach. Call emergency utility services to disconnect at the pole/feeder pillar.
3. **Personnel Safety:** Avoid all direct physical contact with pooled water, wet walls, or metallic fixtures in the affected zone.
4. **Required Expertise:** Any incident involving water ingress near wiring, sockets, appliances, or distribution boards requires dual qualified trades: **Plumbing + Electrical**.
5. **Incident Priority Classification:** **HIGH (Emergency Response within 2 hours)**.

## 2. Standard Electrical Remediation Works
- **Circuit Isolation & Insulation Resistance Testing (Megger Test):** LKR 4,500 – LKR 7,500
- **Damaged Socket / Switch Box Replacement (IP65 rated for wet zones):** LKR 3,500 – LKR 6,000 each
- **Concealed Wiring Rewire (per linear meter in conduit):** LKR 1,200 – LKR 2,000 / m
- **Safety Clearance Certificate by Chartered / Licensed Electrician:** LKR 8,000 – LKR 12,000

---

## 3. Post-Incident Re-energization Protocol
No electrical circuit that has sustained moisture contamination may be energized until:
- Wall cavity moisture levels fall below 12% wood moisture equivalent (WME).
- 500V DC insulation resistance exceeds 1.0 MΩ across all conductors.
- Representative and certified electrician dual sign-off is submitted in AssetBridge AI.
