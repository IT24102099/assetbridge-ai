## 3. Quotation Variance Rules for AI Evaluation
- Any quotation where total material costs exceed catalog benchmarks by more than **20%** must be flagged for manual quantity verification.
- Quotations with labour rates exceeding standard day rate by more than **25%** must provide justification (e.g. emergency after-hours dispatch, confined space hazard).
- Quotations falling within standard rates with valid warranties and verified status receive highest recommendation priority.
