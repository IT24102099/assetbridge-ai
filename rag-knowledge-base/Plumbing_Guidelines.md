# AssetBridge AI — Plumbing Maintenance & Repair Guidelines
**Document ID:** KB-DOC-PLM-003  
**Category:** Plumbing Infrastructure & Standards  

---

## 1. Material Specifications
- **Potable Water Supply:** PN16 or PN20 Random Copolymer Polypropylene (PPR-C) heat-fused pipework, or chlorinated polyvinyl chloride (CPVC) schedule 80. Galvanized iron (GI) pipes should be phased out due to internal scaling and corrosion.
- **Drainage & Soil:** High-density polyethylene (HDPE) or Class 4 PVC with solvent-welded ring-seal sockets.
- **Fixtures & Valves:** Brass ball valves (full bore), ceramic disc mixer cartridges, stainless steel 304 braided flexible hoses.

---

## 2. Standard Inspection Procedure for Reps & Plumbers
1. **Static Pressure Test:** Test pipeline at 1.5 times working pressure (typically 6-8 bar) for a minimum duration of 30 minutes. Pressure drop must not exceed 0.2 bar.
2. **Visual Joint Examination:** Inspect all elbows, tees, unions, and stop valves for weeping, calcium deposits, or stress fractures.
3. **Wall Substrate Assessment:** Check surrounding masonry with acoustic or pinless moisture meter. Mark saturated area boundaries for plaster restoration.

---

## 3. Mandatory Completion Deliverables
- Before and after photos of the replaced pipe joint.
- Pressure gauge reading photo with timestamp.
- Discarded defective parts photographic proof.
- 12-month workmanship guarantee certificate.
